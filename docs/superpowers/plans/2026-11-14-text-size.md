# Text Size Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Larger default text in the native app (14 px floor and +2 px on desktop layouts, 12 px floor on phone layouts) and a saved text size setting with four steps.

**Architecture:** One value type in `pkg/ui`, `TextScale`, owns the ladder and the step; panels and the map renderer ask it for every font size. A step of k is defined as "the 100% layout of a window 1/k the size, multiplied by k": `ui.Workspace` computes at the reduced size and scales its rectangles, and hand-placed chrome multiplies its pixel constants through `TextScale.Px`. The browser host never sets a `TextScale`, and its zero value returns sizes unchanged.

**Tech Stack:** Go 1.26+, Ebitengine v2.10.5 (`text/v2`), ebitenui v0.7.3.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-text-size-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Desktop ladder (layout not compact): sizes below 14 become 14; 14 and above gain 2.
- Phone ladder (compact layout): sizes below 12 become 12; others unchanged.
- Steps are exactly 100, 125, 150, 200 percent. A stored value of 0 or anything else means 100.
- The applied step is the largest step ≤ the chosen one for which `width*100/step >= 320` and `height*100/step >= 480`.
- At 100% the ordinary plan must fit one screen at 390×844 and 1280×850, as today. Existing native tests keep passing without weakened assertions.
- The browser client draws exactly what it draws today, including its map. `pkg/app/host_js.go` and `web/` are not edited.
- `pkg/ui` must not import `pkg/mapview` (`python3 tools/check-boundaries.py`).
- No rules, save, forecast or frame-contract change.
- Lines stay within 120 columns; `gofmt` clean; `go vet ./...` and `GOOS=js GOARCH=wasm go vet ./...` clean.
- Test command for a task unless it says otherwise:
  `GOMEMLIMIT=768MiB GOMAXPROCS=2 go test -p 1 ./pkg/ui ./pkg/render ./pkg/app -count=1`
- Commit once per task on `main` (approved for this plan); never push. Check `git status` first: another session may be editing this checkout, and its work is not ours to revert.

## Review Focus

1. **Window shrinks while a large step is chosen.** Resizing 1920×1080 at 200% down to 1280×850 must drop the applied step to 150% on that same resize, with layout, fonts, camera and map redrawn together. (Task 5 test.)
2. **Bad preference values.** `text_size` of -5, 1, 137 or 100000 in the preferences file behaves as 100% and is rewritten as a valid step on the next save. (Task 1 and Task 7 tests.)
3. **Divider drag at a step other than 100%.** The stored panel width is in 100% units, so dragging at 150% and then switching to 100% leaves the panel at the dragged width divided by 1.5, not a jump to the 800 px clamp. (Task 2 test.)
4. **Map does not redraw.** Changing the step, or crossing between compact and desktop layout, must repaint the cached map scene. (Task 6 test.)
5. **Text fields at large steps.** The name, route-search and help-search fields measure caret and selection positions from their font; at 200% the caret must sit at the click position and the field must be tall enough for its text, in English and Chinese. (Task 3 test.)
